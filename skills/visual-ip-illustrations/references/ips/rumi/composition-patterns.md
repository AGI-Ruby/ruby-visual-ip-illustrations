# Rumi Composition Patterns

## Growth Record

Rumi beside a simple date card, growth marker, or seasonal object. Keep the card in adjacent whitespace and leave the face unobstructed.

## Care Moment

Rumi actively interacts with a brush, comb, toy, blanket, or food puzzle. Use one or two supporting objects and a short Chinese label.

## Portrait Story

Rumi is the clear hero in a warm interior, garden, or storybook scene. Keep the background quiet and leave room for a post title.

## Custom Service Preview

Show Rumi as a visual sample beside a small set of style chips or a simple before/after pair. Keep service text outside the cat silhouette.

## Anti-Repeat Rule

Change the action, supporting object, and camera distance between posts. Do not reuse one template layout as the only visual grammar.
