## Core Style

Use warm, playful editorial pet illustration: creamy off-white space, pale golden fur, sage green, soft coral, toast brown, and restrained navy accents. Prefer rounded hand-drawn shapes, light paper texture, and one clear action per image.

## Must Have

- Rumi remains recognizable as a long-haired golden shaded kitten.
- Preserve the round face, large dark eyes, white chest ruff, ear furnishings, and fluffy tail.
- Keep the scene readable at small Xiaohongshu sizes.
- Use short Chinese labels only when they add meaning.
- Keep generous whitespace around Rumi's face and body.

## Vetoes

- Generic cat face or short-haired cat drift.
- 3D render, plastic fur, hard black cartoon outline, or anime eyes.
- Dense PPT-like diagramming, long paragraphs, or text over the face.
- Unrelated dogs, cats, humans, logos, brand marks, or watermark artifacts.
- Copying the exact layout of another route or project.
