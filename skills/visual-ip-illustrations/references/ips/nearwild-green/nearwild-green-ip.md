# Nearwild Green IP Identity

## Stable markers

- small fox-like local explorer with moss-green fur
- warm cream face, chest, tail tip, and paper accents
- rust-red scarf as the strongest signature color
- paper map and canvas satchel as the product-discovery props
- bright curious eyes and a friendly, observant expression
- compact full-body silhouette that reads at thumbnail scale

## Personality

Nearwild Green is curious before it is promotional. It notices a path, checks the next step, and connects a traveler with the right local help when the situation calls for it. It never pretends to know what has not been verified.

## Action library

- holding open a route map and pointing to the next step
- checking a small brief card before a handoff
- standing between a traveler and a local host with a clear connection line
- carrying a small evidence folder or delivery case
- marking a route checkpoint, backup plan, or trust gate
- looking back at the path after a completed delivery

## Failure modes

- passive mascot decoration
- guide-uniform or travel-agency impersonation
- generic cute animal without map, scarf, or handoff action
- human-like spokesperson with claims of service ownership
- route leakage into Ruby, Tomato, Xiaohei, or licensed characters
