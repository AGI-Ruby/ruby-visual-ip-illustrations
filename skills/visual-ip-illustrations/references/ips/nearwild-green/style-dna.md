# Nearwild Green IP Style DNA

## Palette

- moss green: the character body and local-growth signal
- warm cream: face, tail, paper, and breathing space
- rust red: scarf, handoff emphasis, and small action accents
- muted blue: maps, route lines, and calm trust cues
- warm paper backgrounds: editorial, human, and grounded

## Line and surface

- bold black hand-drawn linework with sparse warm orange/rust accent strokes
- white or transparent negative space and clean silhouette suitable for transparent cutouts and deck corners
- playful editorial storyboard language; avoid glossy 3D toy rendering
- one central action per image; supporting objects stay subordinate

## Visual vetoes

- generic fox, wolf, or fantasy-animal drift
- photorealistic animal, chibi sticker, or plastic 3D emoji drift
- extra logos, badges, text, or invented brand marks
- busy travel postcard collage that hides the action
- saturated neon palette, hard gradients, or dark horror mood
- changing the scarf, map, satchel, or moss-green identity without a route note
