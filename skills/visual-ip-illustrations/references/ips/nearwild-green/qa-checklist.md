# Nearwild Green IP QA Checklist

## Identity

- [ ] Moss-green fox-like explorer is immediately recognizable.
- [ ] Rust-red scarf, paper map, and canvas satchel are present or intentionally omitted with a note.
- [ ] Warm cream face/tail highlights and curious expression remain stable.
- [ ] The character performs the core action; it is not passive decoration.

## Layout

- [ ] Full silhouette is visible when the asset is a cutout.
- [ ] Labels, captions, arrows, and UI elements do not cover the face, scarf, hands, map, satchel, or tail.
- [ ] One action and a small number of supporting objects are enough to read the idea.
- [ ] Deck scenes retain safe whitespace and do not collide with titles or footers.

## Route isolation and claims

- [ ] No drift into Ruby, Tomato, Xiaohei, or licensed-character identity.
- [ ] No claim that the mascot is a real person, guide, partner, or verified service provider.
- [ ] No unapproved logo, badge, or text.
- [ ] Source and route metadata travel with derivatives.

## Delivery

- [ ] Accepted output is saved under `assets/<article-slug>-nearwild-green/`.
- [ ] Deck-only cutouts remain in the Nearwild project visual-IP directory.
- [ ] Prompt, source note, and QA result are recorded when the asset is reused.
