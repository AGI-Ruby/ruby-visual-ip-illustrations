# Nearwild Green IP Composition Patterns

## Preferred structures

1. **Discovery to next step** — Nearwild Green opens a map; a traveler, route marker, and one clear next action sit around it.
2. **Brief handoff** — the mascot checks a compact brief card, then passes it across a simple bridge or line to a local host.
3. **Trust checkpoint** — the mascot places a small verified marker between a public story and a delivery case.
4. **Independent exploration with optional help** — the traveler remains on the main path; a side path leads to a local helper only when needed.
5. **Case close and reuse** — the mascot stores a proof card or feedback note next to the finished route.

## Spatial rules

- Keep the character fully visible and give the map/brief its own breathing room.
- Place labels at the edges or in empty adjacent space; never cover face, scarf, tail, hands, map, or satchel.
- Prefer one hero action plus two or three supporting objects.
- Use route lines, small checkpoints, and handoff arrows sparingly; they should clarify a relationship, not become a diagram wall.
- For deck assets, preserve transparent cutouts and 16:9 editorial scenes separately.

## Anti-repeat rule

Do not reuse the same map-holding pose for every image. Rotate between checking, pointing, carrying, handing off, marking, and reviewing while preserving the stable identity markers.
