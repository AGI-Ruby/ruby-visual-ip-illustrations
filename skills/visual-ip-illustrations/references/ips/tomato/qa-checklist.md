# Tomato QA Checklist

- Route is `tomato`, status is `active`, and output path is `assets/<article-slug>-tomato/`.
- Body is a recognizable red tomato with a green calyx, not a black blob, generic ball or other fruit.
- Face is simple and friendly; the mascot is not a human portrait and contains no Ruby identity markers.
- The mascot performs one clear cognitive action tied to the article or product flow.
- Linework is hand-drawn and editorial; background is white or warm paper with restrained red/green/orange accents.
- Labels, arrows and UI do not cover the calyx, face, body silhouette, hands or main prop.
- No accidental logo repetition, watermark, fake brand text or uncontrolled typography.
- Original source, failed candidate and accepted output remain separately stored.
- Delivery report includes selected route, purpose, saved path and any stability notes.
