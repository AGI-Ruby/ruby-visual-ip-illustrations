# Tomato Style DNA

- Route id: `tomato`
- Route status: `active`
- Output path: `assets/<article-slug>-tomato/`

## Visual language

Warm editorial hand-drawn illustration on a white or very light warm paper background. Use black or deep brown ink for outlines, tomato red for the body and emphasis, leaf green for the calyx and selected states, and restrained orange for connectors and moments of action. Keep the page airy, calm and a little playful, with enough whitespace for adjacent product copy.

The mascot is rounded and approachable. It should perform one legible cognitive action: sort, connect, review, carry, grow, approve, revise or guide. Supporting UI, documents, photos and tables stay secondary to the action.

## Vetoes

- no Xiaohei black blob or black body as the main character
- no Ruby human route leakage, glasses or human face
- no arbitrary fruit species, apple, peach or generic red ball
- no photorealistic tomato, 3D plastic render or glossy emoji style
- no dense poster text, fake UI labels or uncontrolled Chinese typography
- no copied logo-house composition unless the user explicitly asks for the brand scene
- no brand mark pasted repeatedly as a watermark
