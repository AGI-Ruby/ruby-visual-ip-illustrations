# Tomato Composition Patterns

- Route id: `tomato`
- Route status: `active`
- Output path: `assets/<article-slug>-tomato/`

Prefer one of these original structures:

- **Workbench loop:** scattered source material → Tomato organizes it → note, cover, video and approval outputs.
- **Bridge:** supply/brand on one side, creator and real experience on the other, Tomato carries the handoff across a bridge.
- **Garden growth:** small confirmed facts, style choices and feedback become a maintained content garden.
- **Review table:** Tomato holds a draft and a checklist while a human creator makes the final decision.
- **Route map:** Tomato guides a single input through matching, creation, review, publishing and feedback.

Keep the core action in the central or lower-middle whitespace. Place labels at the edge or beside objects, never over the calyx, face, body or main prop. Avoid reusing the exact same scene for every article; carry the identity, change the metaphor.
